Who can use staking?

Staking follows account permissions and token ownership.

ROLE ACCESS
U
Common users · Stake and manage their own tokensAvailable to onboarded users with eligible EMBER in their wallet. They can stake, view rewards, claim, and request an unlock under the pool rules.
E
Expert traders · Same holder accessExperts may stake their own eligible EMBER. Their trading role does not grant control over another user’s stake.
L
Liquidity providers · Same holder accessLiquidity providers may stake eligible available EMBER separately from tokens committed to liquidity pools.
Admin access: configure pools, lock periods, reward rates, and pause rules; review pool activity. Admins should not stake, claim, or withdraw tokens on a user’s behalf.
